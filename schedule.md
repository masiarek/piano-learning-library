# Schedule: 30 minutes a day

## The daily session

| Minutes | Block | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | **Warm-up** | Sit at the right height (forearms level with the keys), curved fingers, loose wrists. Five-finger pattern up and down, each hand, slowly. |
| 3–8 | **Technique** | The technique exercise of the current unit (five-finger patterns, later scales, chords, arpeggios). Hands separately first. |
| 8–13 | **Reading** | One *new* short piece or line, played very slowly, without stopping to fix mistakes. Say the note names aloud; count the beats aloud. |
| 13–25 | **Main piece** | The piece you are learning now. Pick the hardest 2–4 bars, play them hands separately, then together, five clean times. Then play the whole piece once. |
| 25–28 | **Theory** | The theory page of the unit (written exercise, or name the notes/intervals/chords in the piece you just played). |
| 28–30 | **Play for fun** | Something you already know well, at full speed, just for pleasure. Then one line in [practice_log.md](practice_log.md). |

Use a timer. When it rings, move on, even mid-piece: the next day starts where you stopped.

## The week

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Monday | New unit or new piece: listen to the book's recording first (QR code), then the session |
| Tuesday | Normal session |
| Wednesday | Normal session |
| Thursday | Normal session; record yourself playing the main piece on the phone |
| Friday | Normal session; listen back to Thursday's recording, note one thing to fix |
| Saturday | Normal session, plus a "concert": play every piece of the unit once, start to finish, no stopping |
| Sunday | Rest, or 15 minutes of play-for-fun only |

## How fast through the book

- A unit usually takes **1–3 weeks** at this pace. Move on when the unit's checkpoint in [learning_plan.md](learning_plan.md) is met, not when the calendar says so.
- Stuck on one piece for more than two weeks: go on to the next one and come back to it a week later.
- A missed day is not made up by doubling the next; just continue.

## Po polsku, w skrócie

Pół godziny dziennie, podzielone na stałe części: rozgrzewka, technika, czytanie nut a vista, główny utwór (najtrudniejsze takty osobno, potem całość), trochę teorii i na koniec coś dla przyjemności. W tygodniu: poniedziałek nowy materiał, czwartek nagranie, sobota „koncert”, niedziela odpoczynek.

## Auf Deutsch: Stichwörter

Eine feste Einteilung der dreißig Minuten (Aufwärmen, Technik, Vom-Blatt-Spiel, Stück, Theorie) macht jede Übesitzung gleich wirksam.

Aufwärmen, Technik, Vom-Blatt-Spiel, schwierige Takte, Hände getrennt, Hände zusammen, Aufnahme, Wochenplan, Ruhetag

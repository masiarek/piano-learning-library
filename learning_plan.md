# Learning plan

At 30 minutes a day, Book 1 takes roughly **6–12 months** and Book 2 another **9–12 months**. The phases below follow the order of skills in Faber's course; the exact unit titles and page numbers are in the book's table of contents, so write them into the "Units" column once the book arrives.

| Phase | Weeks (about) | Skills | Units | Checkpoint: move on when you can… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Setup** | first days | Bench height, posture, finger numbers 1–5, the keyboard's black-key groups | | find any C, D, E… on the keyboard in under 2 seconds |
| **1. Rhythm and the keyboard** | 1–3 | Quarter, half, whole notes; counting aloud; white-key names | Book 1 | clap and count a 4-bar rhythm without stopping |
| **2. Reading on the staff** | 3–8 | Middle C position, treble and bass clef, the grand staff, steps and skips | Book 1 | name any note within an octave of middle C at sight |
| **3. Intervals and five-finger positions** | 8–16 | 2nds to 5ths, C and G five-finger positions, sharps and flats | Book 1 | play a new 8-bar piece in C position hands together, slowly, first try |
| **4. Chords and harmony** | 16–28 | Major and minor chords, I, IV and V7 chords, playing melody over chords | Book 1 | accompany a melody with I–IV–V7 in C and G |
| **5. Eighth notes, new keys, pedal** | 28–48 | Eighth notes, dotted rhythms, F major, the sustain pedal | end of Book 1 | play the last pieces of Book 1 at the book's tempo with the recording |
| **6. Scales and arpeggios** | Book 2, first half | One-octave major scales with thumb-under, arpeggios, minor keys | Book 2 | play C, G, F major scales hands together at ♩ = 80 |
| **7. Early intermediate** | Book 2, second half | Sixteenth notes, more keys, longer pieces, playing with expression (dynamics, phrasing) | Book 2 | play a full 2-page piece for someone else |

## When to add help

- **A teacher, even 3–5 lessons in phases 0–2**, to check posture and hand shape. A book cannot see your hands; habits set in the first weeks are hard to undo.
- **A teacher again before phase 6**, when the thumb-under for scales starts.

## How sure

| Claim | How sure |
|---|---|
| Daily short practice works better than one long weekly session (spaced practice) | High |
| Book 1 takes about 6–12 months at 30 min/day | Medium; it varies a lot by person |
| The order of skills in the phases matches Faber's course | Medium; written from memory, check against the book's contents |
| Book 2 covers scales, minor keys and sixteenth notes | Medium; from memory |

## Po polsku, w skrócie

Plan w ośmiu fazach: od posadzenia przy klawiaturze i rytmu, przez czytanie nut na pięciolinii, interwały i pozycje pięciopalcowe, akordy, ósemki i pedał, aż do gam i utworów średnio zaawansowanych w drugim tomie. Każda faza kończy się sprawdzianem: przechodzić dalej dopiero, gdy się go zda, a nie wtedy, gdy minie zaplanowany czas.

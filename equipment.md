# Equipment

| Item | When | Note |
|---|---|---|
| *Adult Piano Adventures*, Book 1 (spiral-bound) | now | Spiral lies flat on the music stand |
| *Adult Piano Adventures*, Book 2 | near the end of Book 1 | Look for the spiral-bound edition |
| Keyboard with **88 weighted (hammer-action) keys** and a sustain pedal, or an acoustic piano | before day 1 | Light, unweighted or 61-key keyboards train the wrong finger strength |
| Adjustable bench or chair | before day 1 | Forearms level with the keys |
| Metronome (a phone app is fine) | from phase 2 | |
| Headphones | optional | For practising at any hour |

## Po polsku, w skrócie

Najważniejszy jest instrument: 88 klawiszy z ważoną klawiaturą i pedał. Drugi tom kupić dopiero pod koniec pierwszego, najlepiej na spirali.

## Auf Deutsch: Stichwörter

Eine gewichtete Tastatur mit 88 Tasten ist wichtiger als jedes Lehrbuch.
